# eutelsat.com

> Exceptional connectivity powered by an integrated GEO‑LEO multi‑orbit satellite network.

Eutelsat Group is a global satellite communications operator combining 31 GEO satellites with the Eutelsat OneWeb LEO constellation of more than 600 satellites to deliver resilient, secure connectivity on land, at sea and in the air.

Multi-page, corporate and solutions-focused website with a strong emphasis on multi-orbit innovation, bridging the digital divide, and enterprise-grade satellite connectivity services.
